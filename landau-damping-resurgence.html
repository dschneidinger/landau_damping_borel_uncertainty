Plasma kinetic theory · Maxwellian Langmuir wave

Landau damping is the Borel ambiguity of an integer sequence

The small-k Bohm–Gross series for the Langmuir frequency has integer coefficients and diverges. Summed by the Borel method along any ray just below the real axis, it gives the complete complex Landau root, damping included, at every wavenumber tested from k = 0.2 to 8.

ω² = 1 + 3k² + 6k⁴ + 24k⁶ + 180k⁸ + 1872k¹⁰ + 24192k¹² + 368064k¹⁴ + …
Every coefficient is an integer. This sequence has no match in the OEIS. Units: ωp = λD = vth = 1.

Two readings follow. The exponentially small damping rate, Im ω, is exactly half the Borel ambiguity of this series. And Landau's causal contour, which passes below the pole, is the same thing as choosing to sum on the lower side of the Stokes line. Summing on the upper side gives the complex conjugate.

The structure goes all the way down. Every sector of the transseries is an exact rational series, and the single Stokes jump of the plasma dispersion function predicts each sector from the next, five levels deep. The Landau root also has an infinite sequence of certified exceptional points, where it merges with other kinetic roots, accumulating at k = 0.

What follows from it

Each of these was checked against roots of ε(k, ω) = 0 computed in Arb ball arithmetic. Each root carries an interval-Newton (Krawczyk) certificate that it is the unique zero inside its ball.

  1. A large-order law with no fitted parameters. The growth of the integers an is fixed by the damping sector. Here fh are the exact rational coefficients of the one-instanton series Φ₁ = −ω·Ψ:

    an ≈ (2e−3/2/√π) · Σh (−fh) 2n−h Γ(n + 3/2 − h)

    At n = 1000 this reproduces a 2870-digit integer to 299 digits. That is exactly where the next (two-instanton) sector says it must stop.

  2. An all-orders Landau damping formula with exact rational coefficients:

    γ(k) = −√(π/8) k−3 e−1/(2k²) − 3/2 · Ψ(k²)
    Ψ(g) = 1 − 3g − 27⁄2 g² − 177⁄2 g³ − 7965⁄8 g⁴ − 557361⁄40 g⁵ − …

    In closed form, Ψ = ω(ω − k·dω/dk) e−(ω² − 1 − 3k²)/(2k²), evaluated on the Bohm–Gross series. Optimally truncated, it matches the certified damping rate to 2×10⁻³¹ at k = 0.08 and 5×10⁻²⁰ at k = 0.1. The textbook formula is off by 2.0% and 3.3% at those points.

  3. The Stokes multiplier is σ = 1, not 2. Kowalenko (Phys. Lett. A 337, 408 (2005)) states that “a proper treatment of the Stokes phenomenon reveals a factor of 2 missing in the standard result for Landau damping.” For the field-free Maxwellian, doubling the standard rate is off by 100% against the certified roots at every k tested.

    The resurgent picture shows where a 2 can enter. σ = 2 is correct only when paired with the lateral sum of the series, which carries an imaginary part of −½ the jump. Pairing σ = 2 with the real (truncated or median) series counts the damping twice.

    I read only that paper's abstract. Whether its claim is meant to cover the field-free limit is unconfirmed.

  4. The whole resurgent tower closes on one Stokes jump. Write the root as a transseries W = W₀ + Σm σm um, where sector m carries e−m/(2k²). Every sector reduces to an exact rational power series Ψm(k²), computed by Lagrange inversion:

    Ψ₂ = −½ + 3g + 9g² + 54g³ + 693g⁴ + …, Ψ₃ = −⅜ + 63⁄16 g + 39⁄64 g² + …

    Z′ has a single Stokes jump, σ → σ + 2. That alone fixes how each sector's discontinuity feeds the next, Disc um = Σj C(m+j, m) 2j um+j. So the coefficients of sector m are predicted by sector m+1, with Stokes multipliers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and nothing fitted. With 800 terms per sector, all five levels match to between 3×10⁻¹¹⁸ and 10⁻¹¹⁶ at n = 399. Shifting any multiplier by one breaks the match.

  5. Exceptional points accumulate at k = 0. At an exceptional point the Langmuir root merges with another kinetic root (ε = ∂ε/∂ω = 0). The first 30 are certified by interval arithmetic, and the physical Landau root, continued from real k, runs into every one of them. They approach the origin along the imaginary k² axis:

    k*ₙ² ≈ i/(4πn), with ζₙ² = ln(4i√π ζₙ⁵) − 2πi n

    The second formula uses the off-line Stokes multiplier σ = 2. It predicts the certified points with relative error falling from 2×10⁻² at n = 1 to 5×10⁻⁵ at n = 30. So the Landau root is not analytic in any neighbourhood of k = 0.

Controls

  • planted truthThe identical pipeline, applied to the bare asymptotic series of Z′, recovers the exact Z′(ζ)/(2x) to 10⁻⁷⁹ or better at x = 0.02, 0.05 and 0.1, including its exponentially small imaginary part.
  • wrong sheetRays above the axis return conj(ωL²) and fail against ωL². At k = 0.3 the error is 0.059 against the root and 3×10⁻²⁰ against its conjugate.
  • corruptionMultiplying the integers an for n ≥ 40 by 1 + 10⁻⁶ destroys the match at every k tested (error ≥ 10¹⁷).

Derivation

With g = k², W = ω² and ζ = ω/(√2 k), the dispersion relation is ε = 1 − Z′(ζ)/(2g) = 0. For large ζ:

Z′(ζ) = Σn (2n+1)!! / (2n ζ2n+2) − 2iσ√π ζ e−ζ²

Perturbative sector. Put x = g/W = 1/(2ζ²). The asymptotic part becomes P(x) = Σ (2n+1)!! xn+1 = g. So x(g) is the series reversion of P, and W₀ = g/x(g). The coefficients are integers because P has integer coefficients and leading coefficient 1.

One-instanton sector. Write G(W, g) = W⁻¹ D(g/W). Because g/W is x(g) itself, the inverse-function rule gives 1/∂WG = −(W − gW′). Then

δω = −iσ√(π/8) k−3 ω(ω − k vg) e−ω²/(2k²)

with vg = dω/dk on the Bohm–Gross series. This is the classic weak-damping formula. What is new is that it is the exact one-instanton sector of a resurgent transseries, so all of its rational coefficients carry meaning.

Stokes structure.

  • Z = i√π e−ζ² erfc(−iζ) is entire. Its asymptotic series has a single Stokes line on real ζ, with jump 2i√π e−ζ².
  • The upper-half-plane lateral Borel sum, analytically continued, is exactly Z.
  • Lateral Borel summation commutes with the algebra and the implicit-function step, because 1-summable series form a differential algebra closed under composition and implicit functions (Balser, Formal Power Series and Linear Systems of Meromorphic ODEs).
  • So the damped root is the lateral Borel sum of W₀. Re ωL² and Im ωL² are the half-sum and half-difference of the two lateral sums.
  • The large-order law follows from a Cauchy integral over the discontinuity, which is twice the one-instanton term.

What is and is not established

rigorous
The integers an and the rational coefficients of every sector Ψm are exact. The roots ωL(k) and the 30 exceptional points carry interval-Newton certificates.
also numerical
That the Landau root reaches each exceptional point comes from Newton tracking along a path at 160+ bits, not from a certificate. The tower's large-order agreements are high-precision evaluations of exact quantities, not interval bounds.
numerical
The Borel–Padé sums are uncertified numerics. Their agreement with the certified roots improves steadily up to N = 160, reaching 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻²⁷ depending on k, then levels off.
theorem
The identity for all k holds modulo the standard summability-closure results cited above. There is a proof sketch, not a full proof.
novelty
The check was limited. The OEIS has no match for the sequence. Web searches found the Kowalenko paper but no resurgent treatment of the Landau root, no large-order law and no all-orders Ψ. Exceptional points of Vlasov dispersion relations are a known general idea (for example arXiv:2608.19624, on magnetized plasmas), but I found no account of this infinite sequence or its accumulation law. A proper literature audit is needed before any priority claim.
scope
This is a structural result about linear Landau damping of the Maxwellian Langmuir wave. It changes no practical damping rate: the textbook formula was already adequate wherever the damping is small.

Reproduce

The scripts live in project_1/ of a BootLoops checkout. They need Python 3.12 with python-flint and mpmath. Each runs in seconds to a few minutes.

python series.py                          # integers and the one-instanton series
python large_order.py 1000                # large-order law, ~20 s
python damping.py                         # all-orders damping vs certified roots
python borel.py 120 80 "-0.25,-0.7,-1.2"  # Borel sums vs certified roots
python controls.py planted                # also: sheet, corrupt, converge
python transseries.py 800 5               # resurgence tower, five levels (~3 min)
python ep.py 30                           # certified exceptional points

Computed with Arb ball arithmetic (python-flint) and mpmath, inside the BootLoops toolkit environment.
